01 / ABOUTMy Background.

Hi! I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Purdue ECE, co-advised by Prof. Yaobin Chen and Prof. Ziran Wang.

I received my bachelor degree in information engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Siheng Chen. I have also spent a wonderful summer at the Department of Computer Science, Purdue University as a research intern, advised by Prof. Aniket Bera.

My research focuses on autonomous driving and computer vision, with an emphasis on multi-agent intelligence, vision-language-action (VLA) models, and foundation models for embodied autonomy. I am broadly interested in building efficient, trustworthy, and human-aligned autonomous systems that can perceive, reason, collaborate, and act in complex real-world environments.

  1. IEEE IoT J.
    VLM-DC: Vision Language Model for Automotive Data Collection
    Can Cui*, Juntong Peng*, Sungyeon Park, Yongkang Liu, Seyhan Ucar, Satoshi Kitai, Ahmadreza Moradipari, Maryam Khabazi, and Ziran Wang
    IEEE Internet of Things Journal, 2026
    Abs DOI HTML

    We introduce VLM-DC, a two-module vision-language model framework for intelligent and targeted automotive data collection. The framework combines cloud-edge multi-agent collaboration with a distributed VLM architecture and knowledge distillation to improve scene selection while reducing storage, model, and bandwidth requirements.
